## Controls

Current climate, accounting, target-setting and nature-framework claims use the primary sources in the course register with a 29 August 2026 cut-off. Jakub Malich and Duncan Lee material is presented as practitioner perspective. The methods use IPCC hazard–exposure–vulnerability and residual-risk concepts, TNFD LEAP, ECHA substitution guidance, ISO 14040 principles and the European Commission Environmental Footprint method. The course teaches screening and literacy, not formal climate modelling, TNFD assessment, chemical qualification or ISO-compliant LCA practice.
