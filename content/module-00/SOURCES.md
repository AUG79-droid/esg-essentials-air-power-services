## Use notes

All source concepts are paraphrased. The Enron chronology is condensed to governance learning logic and checked against SEC, US Supreme Court/GovInfo and statutory records listed in the course source register. The source’s historical interpretation of Enron’s place in ESG is not presented as established fact.

## Gap controls

Externalities are explained at beginner conceptual level and now connect to the framework-neutral materiality process in Modules 02 and 08. That process is supported by GRI 3, EFRAG IG 1 and IFRS S1 but is not presented as one universal reporting methodology.
