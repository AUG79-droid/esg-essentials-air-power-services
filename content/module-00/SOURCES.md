# Module 00 — Sources and further reading

Use these links to check the concepts and case chronology introduced in this module. They open the public page maintained by the relevant institution.

## ESG, impacts and governance

- [GRI Standards — Universal Standards, including GRI 3: Material Topics](https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/) — impact reporting and identification of material topics.
- [IFRS Foundation — IFRS S1 supporting materials](https://www.ifrs.org/supporting-implementation/supporting-materials-for-ifrs-sustainability-disclosure-standards/ifrs-s1/) — investor-focused sustainability-related financial information.
- [EFRAG — ESRS implementation guidance documents](https://www.efrag.org/en/projects/esrs-implementation-guidance-documents) — implementation guidance, including double materiality.
- [G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance 2023](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2023/09/g20-oecd-principles-of-corporate-governance-2023_60836fcb.html) — roles, oversight, disclosure and accountability.
- [OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Business Conduct](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/due-diligence-guidance-for-responsible-business-conduct.html) — risk-based due diligence across business relationships.

## Enron and the governance case

- [US SEC — What did the Sarbanes–Oxley Act do?](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/office-hours-gary-gensler-sarbanes-oxley) — SEC retrospective linking Enron and other scandals to the 2002 reforms.
- [US SEC — Statement regarding the Andersen conviction](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2002-89-sec-statement-regarding-andersen-case-conviction) — contemporary SEC record of the 2002 conviction and practice consequences.
- [US Supreme Court via GovInfo — Arthur Andersen LLP v. United States, 544 U.S. 696 (2005)](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/USREPORTS-544/USREPORTS-544-696/context) — judgment reversing the conviction because of defective jury instructions.
- [US SEC — 2002 Annual Report](https://www.sec.gov/pdf/annrep02/ar02full.pdf) — contemporary account of enforcement, reporting, audit oversight and Sarbanes–Oxley implementation.

## About the learning scenario

The supplier case and ESG Compass are fictional exercises created for learning. They do not describe Airbus policy, an actual supplier, an actual procurement decision or a classified process. Their purpose is to practise distinguishing a claim from evidence, making uncertainty visible and assigning follow-up actions.
