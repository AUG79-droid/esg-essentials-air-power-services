# Module 00 — Interactive activity: ESG Compass

## Purpose

Build a defensible next step for the fictional Supplier A and Supplier B case described in the module. The activity does not ask learners to identify a supposedly “sustainable supplier”, and it does not replace technical, legal, quality, cost or schedule requirements.

## Evidence available to the learner

- Both suppliers passed the same technical and quality gates.
- Stock covers 11 weeks and the award meeting is in 10 days.
- Supplier A costs 8% more and delivers in 9 weeks. It traces 95% of material mass and provides 24 months of workforce indicators, but its energy-intensity data is not independently verified.
- Supplier B costs 12% less and delivers in 5 weeks. It provides only a tier-one self-declaration, an audit summary without scope, date or sample, and no information about worker grievance channels.

## Task

1. Select at least four signals that must inform the decision.
2. Choose a provisional route: advance A with conditions; request comparable evidence from both suppliers within the available window; advance B only after closing the gaps; or escalate a potentially non-negotiable requirement.
3. Evaluate the map and compare the recommendation with the reasoning standard.

## Reasoning standard

A defensible recommendation keeps technical, commercial and operational evidence in view, treats unsupported claims as unknowns, defines specific conditions, and assigns an owner and review date. More than one route may be reasonable, but uncertainty must not be presented as proof.
