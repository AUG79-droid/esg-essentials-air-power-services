## Treatment

Matt Friedman, Vivian Seo, Jakub Malich and Duncan Lee are paraphrased practitioner perspectives, not endorsements. The sources strongly support severe-risk indicators, vulnerable workers, supply-chain opacity, social-audit limitations and the value of stakeholder insight.

International definitions and due-diligence expectations use ILO, OHCHR and OECD sources with a 29 August 2026 cut-off. EU examples use the consolidated CSDDD and Forced Labour Regulation without implying universal application. The course includes an essentials-level HRDD cycle, people-risk severity, remedy distinction, buyer-practice test, UNGP grievance-effectiveness loop, safeguarded-interview awareness and community-impact screen. These do not replace applicable law, formal impact assessment, professional investigation or auditor protocols.

The electronics scenario and Supply Chain Investigation are `AIR POWER SERVICES LEARNING SCENARIO` content, not actual Airbus procedures or supplier facts.
