# Module 02 — Visual Explainers

## Visual 1 — Finance terminology map

**Purpose:** Give non-finance learners a stable conceptual map while acknowledging evolving usage.  
**Elements:** Sustainable finance umbrella; nested/overlapping green, environmental and climate finance; separate lenses for ESG integration, impact and responsible lending.  
**Labels:** “Broad process,” “Environmental focus,” “Intended measurable outcome,” “Product/strategy terms may overlap.”  
**Takeaway:** Similar words can have different purposes; always inspect definition and evidence.  
**Interaction:** Toggle the generic concept and a dated jurisdiction/product layer, with links to the governing source.

## Visual 2 — ESG-to-value transmission channels

**Purpose:** Show causal logic without promising performance.  
**Elements:** ESG issue → management response → cash-flow / company-specific / systematic channel → financial assessment; uncertainty and confounding factors surround pathway.  
**Takeaway:** A plausible channel must be evidenced; ESG quality is not a guaranteed return.  
**Interaction:** Build a pathway for water, workforce continuity or governance.

## Visual 3 — Investment approach spectrum

**Purpose:** Compare mechanisms.  
**Elements:** Exclude, integrate, select peers, engage/steward, thematic, impact; rows for objective, action, evidence and limitation.  
**Takeaway:** Approaches are not interchangeable.  
**Interaction:** Match an investor objective to an approach and receive boundary feedback.
