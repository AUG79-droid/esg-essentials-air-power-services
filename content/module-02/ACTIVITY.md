# Module 02 — Interactive Activity: Investment Decision Lab

## Scenario

In this **AIR POWER SERVICES LEARNING SCENARIO**, a committee has constrained capital for an energy retrofit, repair capability, supplier traceability or “do minimum.”

## Learner task and actions

The learner reviews project cards, tests assumptions, selects a portfolio and writes conditions. Each card separates financial estimate, ESG pathway, evidence quality, implementation dependency and proposed outcome measure. Learners may fund, defer, pilot or reject projects and must explain the counterfactual.

## Feedback

- Funding by project title triggers label/evidence feedback.
- Selecting only the shortest payback reveals resilience and lifecycle information.
- Ignoring cost/schedule reveals that ESG complements rather than replaces finance.
- Treating modeled benefits as achieved outcomes requires a verification plan.
- Diversifying funds without materiality prompts focus on decision objectives.

## Learning logic and expected outcome

Multiple portfolios can pass. A strong outcome links capital to material pathways, makes assumptions visible, protects non-negotiable controls and defines evidence for post-investment review.
