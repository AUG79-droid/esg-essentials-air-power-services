# Module 03 — Interactive Activity: Greenwashing Detective

## Scenario and task

Learners investigate the three **AIR POWER SERVICES LEARNING SCENARIO** claims in `MODULE.md`. For each, they sort cards into `CLAIM`, `EVIDENCE`, `METRIC`, `TARGET`, `OUTCOME` or `UNKNOWN`, then choose: support, narrow/qualify, request evidence, or reject/escalate. For the surviving metric they reconstruct source, owner, definition, boundary, method, calculation, control, review and change history, then judge whether it is ready for internal review—not whether it is “assured.”

## Feedback and learning logic

- A target placed as outcome is corrected.
- A certificate placed as complete evidence reveals its scope and date.
- “Zero waste” without destination evidence triggers boundary feedback.
- Rejecting every claim prompts calibrated skepticism: identify what would substantiate it.
- Accepting a narrow improvement as whole-lifecycle superiority reveals selective disclosure.
- Missing version history prevents unexplained restatement; confusing management review with external assurance triggers scope feedback.

The expected outcome is a revised, evidence-bounded statement and a short evidence request—not merely a “greenwashing/not greenwashing” verdict.
