# Module 07 — Interactive Activity: Boardroom Challenge

## Scenario

The **AIR POWER SERVICES LEARNING SCENARIO** combines readiness pressure, a proposed supplier, technical uncertainty, cost/schedule, social-risk indicators, chemical-treatment implications and reputation. Safety, airworthiness, security and technical authority are locked non-negotiable constraints.

## Learner task

Sort decisions into operational, senior-management, technical-authority and board/reserved oversight. Build the information pack, identify missing evidence, surface conflicts and record conditional actions.

## Feedback

Bypassing technical authority is blocked. Sending every detail to the board prompts accountability feedback. Hiding severe people-risk inside an aggregate rating prompts materiality feedback. A recommendation without dissent, counterfactual or monitoring is incomplete. A delay decision without readiness consequence is also incomplete.

## Expected outcome

The learner assigns authority correctly, escalates material uncertainty, preserves mandatory gates and produces a transparent conditional decision with ownership and review.
