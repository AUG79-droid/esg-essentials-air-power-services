# Module 07 — Visual Explainers

## Visual 1 — Board–management authority map

**Purpose:** Separate direction/oversight from execution.  
**Elements:** Board, committees, executive management, operational management, technical authority and assurance. Decision, oversight, advice and escalation arrows are distinct.  
**Takeaway:** Consultation does not blur who decides.  
**Interaction:** Route scenario decisions to the appropriate level; display a persistent reminder that actual authority comes from the entity’s governing documents, delegations and applicable law.

## Visual 2 — Board information funnel

**Purpose:** Show how detail becomes decision-ready evidence without losing warnings.  
**Elements:** Source evidence → management analysis → executive recommendation → board pack; dissent, severe impact and control-failure lanes bypass aggregation.  
**Takeaway:** Material exceptions must survive summarization.  
**Interaction:** Learner builds a one-screen board view and sees what was wrongly omitted.

## Visual 3 — Ethical decision canvas

**Purpose:** Structure judgment without a single score.  
**Elements:** facts/unknowns, authority, rights/stakeholders, constraints, consequences, alternatives, conflicts/incentives, reversibility, decision, monitoring.  
**Takeaway:** Ethical reasoning is traceable and conditional.  
**Interaction:** Completing one field reveals consequences for later fields.

## Visual 4 — Constructive challenge conditions

**Purpose:** Explain why independence requires process.  
**Elements:** skills, diverse perspective, access to evidence, time, chair behavior, protected dissent, independent advice and follow-through.  
**Takeaway:** Formal composition is necessary in context but not sufficient for effective challenge.  
**Interaction:** Toggle missing conditions and see how information risk grows.
