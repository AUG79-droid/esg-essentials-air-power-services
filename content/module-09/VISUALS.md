# Module 09 — Visual Explainers

## Visual 1 — Capstone systems dashboard

**Purpose:** Hold the integrated decision without a single score.  
**Elements:** technical locked gates; evidence confidence; environmental pathways; rights-holder indicators; enterprise risks; governance/authority; schedule/cost; residual uncertainty.  
**Takeaway:** Dimensions remain visible and severe/mandatory issues cannot be averaged away.  
**Interaction:** Each stage progressively populates the dashboard.

## Visual 2 — Evidence provenance graph

**Purpose:** Trace claim to record, method, control and reviewer.  
**Elements:** claim, metric, data source, transformation, boundary, owner, independent check, decision use.  
**Takeaway:** Credibility is a chain.  
**Interaction:** Learner selects evidence and sees missing provenance.

## Visual 3 — Authority and escalation control room

**Purpose:** Show multiple valid authorities.  
**Elements:** programme, procurement, quality, compliance, environmental specialist, technical authority, executive and board/reserved oversight.  
**Takeaway:** “Approved” must identify what, by whom and within what authority.  
**Interaction:** Route decisions; mandatory gates cannot be bypassed.

## Visual 4 — Option and uncertainty matrix

**Purpose:** Compare options without false aggregation.  
**Elements:** options as columns; technical constraint, evidence, environmental lifecycle, people risk, resilience, cost/schedule, reversibility, conditions and unknowns as rows.  
**Takeaway:** A conditional sequence may be stronger than a single static option.  
**Interaction:** Learner adds conditions and sees residual risk, not a green score.

## Visual policy

Use premium dark-navy aerospace engineering/control-room visuals, technical network lines and restrained cyan/amber status. No aircraft-with-nature composition. If a later aircraft visual is authorized, only A400M or Eurofighter may appear in a hangar, factory, maintenance, engineering, air-base or technical-operations context.
