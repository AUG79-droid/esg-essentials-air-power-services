# Module 06 — Interactive Activity: Governance Failure Room

## Scenario

The **AIR POWER SERVICES LEARNING SCENARIO** is a fictional programme with a green dashboard, unresolved supplier evidence, ambiguous responsibility, delayed escalation and schedule-heavy incentives.

## Learner task

Investigate six stations: objective, authority, control, evidence, information and incentives. At each, classify the failure and propose a corrective action.

## Actions

1. Reconstruct the real decision from fragmented records.
2. Assign risk owner, control owner, decision authority and independent challenger.
3. Identify policy, procedure and operating control artifacts.
4. Test whether controls were designed, executed and effective.
5. Restore omitted warnings to the dashboard.
6. Choose escalation timing and authority.
7. Define corrective action and evidence of closure.
8. Separate risk capacity, appetite, tolerance and operational limit, then identify any non-compensable gate.

## Feedback

Feedback distinguishes unclear authority, weak design, non-execution, ineffective control, incomplete evidence, misclassification, incentive conflict and delayed escalation. Learners who merely add another checklist are asked which causal failure it addresses. Learners who assign all responsibility to internal audit are reminded that management owns operations and controls.

Any attempt to offset failed safety, airworthiness, security, legal or mandatory technical requirements with an ESG score is rejected. The learner must remove the infeasible option or escalate through the correct authority.

## Expected outcome

A coherent governance repair: corrected status, explicit ownership, evidence criteria, timely escalation, balanced incentives, protected dissent and verified corrective action. Technical approval remains a separate non-negotiable authority.
