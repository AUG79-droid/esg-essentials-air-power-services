# Module 04 — Interactive Activity: Stakeholder Lens

## Scenario

The training-access scenario in `MODULE.md` is an **AIR POWER SERVICES LEARNING SCENARIO**.

## Learner task and actions

Learners map stakeholders and rights-holders; separate rights, needs, preferences and operational constraints; inspect participation, scheduling, nomination, equipment and interview evidence; identify missing voices; and redesign the process without weakening valid standards. They define an engagement route, explain how feedback can influence the decision and build one privacy-aware input → activity → output → outcome indicator chain.

## Feedback

- “Same invitation means fair access” reveals scheduling and nomination barriers.
- Removing competence criteria triggers clarification that equity is not lower standards.
- Using identity averages alone reveals privacy, sample-size and intersectional limitations.
- Assuming non-use of grievance channels means no problem reveals trust and retaliation risks.
- Excluding contractors prompts examination of employment status, role and applicable protections without inventing legal obligations.
- Collecting identifiable demographic data “just in case” prompts purpose limitation, minimisation, access and retention controls.
- Sending the final decision without a response route is classified as communication, not consultation.

## Expected outcome

A revised process with fair access routes, accountable nomination criteria, accessible scheduling/material/equipment, safe feedback, privacy controls and outcome review.
