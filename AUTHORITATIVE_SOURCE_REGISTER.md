# Authoritative Source Register

## Purpose and status

This register supports the Phase 4A review of claims previously labelled `CURRENT VERIFICATION REQUIRED`. The verification cut-off is **29 August 2026**. Sources are primary institutional, regulatory, legislative, judicial or standard-setter publications. A source establishes what the named institution or instrument says; it does not establish that a rule applies to every learner, entity or jurisdiction.

## Use controls

- **Date-sensitive:** A02–A14, A19–A23. Recheck before publication and whenever the course is materially revised.
- **Jurisdiction-sensitive:** A05, A07–A14, A19–A23. A citation does not establish applicability.
- **Durable historical/normative basis:** A01, A15–A18, A24–A27, while interpretations and implementation can still evolve.
- Corporate or provider interviews in the supplied material are treated as time-bounded practitioner perspectives, not authoritative evidence of current commitments or methodology.
- Transcript statistics and named cases without traceable primary provenance were removed; they are not silently validated by a thematically related source.
