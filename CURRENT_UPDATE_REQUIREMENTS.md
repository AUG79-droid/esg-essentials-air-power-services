# ESG Essentials — Current Update Requirements

## Purpose and publication rule

The supplied transcripts are historical source material. This register identifies claims that must be verified against current authoritative sources immediately before authoring/publication. It deliberately does **not** state current legal requirements. Every verified claim should record source, jurisdiction, publication/effective date, access date, applicability, owner and next review date.

## Review workflow before release

1. Freeze intended jurisdictions and learner population.
2. Assign each register line to a legal, sustainability, HR, procurement or governance owner.
3. Verify against primary/authoritative sources; archive citation and version metadata.
4. Record whether the source transcript remains accurate, needs a dated qualification, or must be replaced.
5. Separate durable theory from volatile “current state” cards in the build.
6. Conduct legal/compliance review of all “must,” “required,” “prohibited,” “reportable” and deadline language.
7. Repeat immediately before publication and set a scheduled content-review cadence.

## Known transcript issues to correct during authoring

- Broken characters (for example degree symbols and em dashes) must be normalized.
- Duplicate phrases and interface transcript fragments must be removed.
- The Enron bankruptcy date appears misstated in the transcript and must be verified.
- Expired forward-looking dates (notably 2025 projections) must not be republished as future claims.
- Statistics with no original citation should be omitted unless their authoritative provenance is recovered.
