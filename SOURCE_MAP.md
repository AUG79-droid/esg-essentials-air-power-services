## Consolidation logic

- Sources 1–3, 5, 16, 35 and 48 are retained as orientation material but consolidated to avoid repetitive pillar introductions.
- The natural-resource sequence (7–15) becomes a dependency–impact–risk–response model rather than a catalogue of environmental problems.
- Finance sources 16–34 are reorganized around capital allocation, materiality, ratings/reporting limitations and evidence quality. Provider-specific claims are labeled as practitioner perspectives.
- DEI sources 36–43 are retained, with legal and cultural claims flagged for jurisdictional verification and with emphasis on fair systems, accessibility, worker voice and respectful dialogue.
- Sources 45–47 form a coherent supply-chain human-rights sequence: identify vulnerability, examine purchasing/contracting conditions, evaluate evidence, and escalate/remediate.
- Governance sources 48–54 are split between foundational governance systems (Module 06) and board/oversight decision practice (Module 07).
- Sources 55–57 supply the implementation spine for Modules 08–09, supported by concepts from every earlier module.
