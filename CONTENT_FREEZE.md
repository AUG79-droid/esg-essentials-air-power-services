## Source and currency boundary

The authoritative source registers and module source files are frozen as of this date. Scenario content is explicitly educational. Real cases remain source-bounded and must retain their qualification. No producer may silently update law, standards, company facts, market claims, ratings, emissions factors or other time-sensitive information. A proposed currency update reopens change control.

## Change control

Allowed without reopening substantive approval: spelling, punctuation, broken-link repair, accessible labelling, responsive reflow, equivalent alt text, and implementation corrections that preserve exact meaning. All other changes require an entry stating requester, rationale, affected IDs/files, source basis, assessment/visual impact, reviewer and approval date. Changes to a correct answer, material claim, named case, quantitative statement, source, activity consequence, module completion rule or Decision Lab state require content-owner reapproval before release.
