## Content architecture created

Each module contains:

- `MODULE.md` — opening, objectives, substantive theory, APS translation, case/practitioner perspective, myths, decision lens and close;
- `VISUALS.md` — native HTML/CSS/JS explainer specifications with purpose, elements, labels, takeaway and interaction;
- `ACTIVITY.md` — reasoning scenario, learner actions, feedback logic and expected outcome;
- `KNOWLEDGE_CHECK.md` — five formative questions with correct and incorrect-option explanations;
- `SOURCES.md` — exact supplied filenames, practitioner treatment, marker controls and original-content notes.

## Substantive source limitations found during writing

1. Many transcript statistics lack inline provenance and cannot safely become learner-facing facts.
2. Several projections use dates that are now past or near-term, especially 2025.
3. Reporting, rating, finance and regulatory descriptions are highly time-sensitive and sometimes jurisdictionally ambiguous.
4. Provider interviews are valuable for practical logic but describe provider/investor perspectives rather than neutral universal methods.
5. The source explains climate mitigation more strongly than physical-risk assessment and adaptation.
6. Environmental topics are rich individually but lack a formal lifecycle method for evaluating trade-offs.
7. Social sources cover DEI well but do not provide a complete rights-holder-centered due-diligence/remedy framework.
8. Social indicators are introduced without a complete privacy, data-quality or people-analytics method.
9. Enron chronology/legal details need authoritative correction and verification; its role in the origin of ESG is interpretive.
10. The source is general business content; all aerospace translations required original, clearly labeled pedagogy and will need APS subject-matter review.
