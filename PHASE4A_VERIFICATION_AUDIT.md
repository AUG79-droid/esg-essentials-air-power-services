## What changed materially

### Science, statistics and projections

- Climate attribution now uses the IPCC AR6 Synthesis wording and states the assessed period.
- Untraceable transcript totals and projections covering water, oceans, sea level, pollution health burden, waste, recycling, extraction, methane, circularity, renewable shares and investment needs were not “updated” from adjacent sources. They were removed or converted into evidence-quality instructions.
- The Cape Town example is qualitative only. The Jakarta litigation detail was removed because the exact primary case and current procedural status were not established.

### Standards and regulatory status

- GHG Protocol source documents are named while its Corporate Suite update is disclosed.
- SBTi Corporate Net-Zero Standard v2 is identified as published in June 2026, with validation scheduled from 2027; v1.3.1 remains relevant to 2026 target setting.
- TNFD is correctly described as a voluntary recommendation framework; LEAP is optional guidance, not certification or a universal legal duty.
- IFRS S1/S2, the December 2025 IFRS S2 amendments, GRI Universal Standards, CSRD changes and the Commission’s 3 July 2026 revised ESRS adoption are dated. The course warns that EU delegated-act legal effect depends on scrutiny and Official Journal publication.
- EU ESG ratings, Taxonomy, European Green Bond and ESMA fund-name rules are presented as bounded examples, not global definitions.
- The consolidated EU CSDDD reflects amendments through 18 March 2026. EU forced-labour product rules and whistleblower rules are not presented as universal or simultaneously applicable.
- Assurance text no longer implies that limited assurance, reasonable assurance, internal review, certification and verification are interchangeable. Applicability is routed to the engagement criteria, current standard and jurisdiction.

### Social and governance claims

- UNGP, OECD and ILO expectations are separated from binding local law.
- Forced labour uses the ILO Convention No. 29 elements and current identification resources; indicators are described as clues, not automatic legal findings.
- DEI, privacy, employment, worker interviews, retaliation, community consultation, director duties, board structure and delegated authority now carry operational applicability gates rather than unresolved warning labels.
- Practitioner/company/provider interviews are expressly time-bounded and cannot evidence current commitments or methodology.

### Enron

- The learner text now records Enron’s 2 December 2001 Chapter 11 filing, Arthur Andersen’s 2002 conviction and cessation of practice before the SEC, the 2005 US Supreme Court reversal based on defective jury instructions, and the July 2002 Sarbanes–Oxley response.
- It avoids a misleading procedural shorthand: Andersen’s conviction was reversed; that legal outcome is distinct from denying the wider audit and governance failures documented in official records.
- It also rejects the source-era assertion that Enron “started” modern ESG as settled history. Enron is taught as a governance-failure case and one catalyst among several for US reporting and audit reform.

## Verification controls and residual risks

1. **Cut-off risk:** EU sustainability regulation and standard-setting are moving quickly. A pre-publication delta check is required after 29 August 2026, especially for ESRS legal effect, CSRD/CSDDD scope, Taxonomy delegated acts, ESG-rating implementation, SBTi transition and ISSA 5000 adoption.
2. **Applicability risk:** The course cannot determine duties for an unidentified APS entity, reporting period, product or jurisdiction. Legal, finance, privacy, people, procurement and assurance owners must complete the applicability map.
3. **Source-version risk:** URLs may be updated while documents retain or change versions. Production should record accessed copies/version identifiers in its controlled repository.
4. **Corporate-currentness risk:** Interview material remains valuable only as practitioner perspective at the time of recording. No current AIA, MSCI or other corporate commitment is asserted.
5. **Deliberate Phase 4A boundary:** The 28 `AUTHORITATIVE EXPANSION REQUIRED` method gaps remain. This phase performed only the small amount of verification necessary to avoid conflicting or misleading current claims.
