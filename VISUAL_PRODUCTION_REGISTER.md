# Visual Production Register

## Production principles

The 46 approved explainers are production-bound native diagrams. They must carry teaching content, remain readable without animation and never depend on generated imagery. Generated module heroes are listed separately after the V-register. Use dark navy foundations, cyan/electric-blue information paths, amber uncertainty and restrained metallic detail. Never use leaf/globe/forest shorthand.
